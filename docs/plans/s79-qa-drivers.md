## The pivot
**The driver choice lives in `qa-drivers/SKILL.md` and is read by `qa`, while `/po-qa` only stops on what it can see from files (surface `—`, or `screen` with `Dev` at `—`).** Ways it could be wrong:
- The base directory of a skill preloaded in a subagent is not announced in some sessions → every QA ends blocked. Compare with a real `/po-qa` run after install; ADR-0057 makes it a visible blocked cause, never a guess.
- `qa.md` and `po-qa.md` disagree on when `Dev` matters (one scoped to `screen`, the other not) → a CLI story stopped by `/po-qa` before `qa` sees it. Compare the two gate sections line by line; `qa_runs_without_a_screen` checks both.
- The lint's driver-value check refuses a legitimate future profile (native iOS with no driver) → ADR-0057 admits `—`; compare with s85's criterion.
